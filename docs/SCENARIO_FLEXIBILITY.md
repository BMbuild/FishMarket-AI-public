# Scenario demo: any species, your own question, your situation

## Species
- **Any species can be picked in any region.** The list is ordered in three groups:
  1. **Suits your operation:** species whose usual gear matches the operation. For example, a pot and trap fisher in
     Alaska sees king, snow and Tanner crab, Pacific cod, sablefish and octopus first. The gear rules are in
     `fishmarket_data/registry/species_keywords.csv` and `operation_gears.csv`.
  2. **The rest of the region:** the hand-made demo species, every species NOAA records as landed commercially in the
     matching real region (`data_real/crosswalk/scenario_noaa_species.csv`, rebuilt with
     `python tools/make_scenario_species.py`), and the farmed species in `farmed_species_extra.csv`.
  3. **Other regions:** everything else.
- You can also **type any name**.
- **Generated data:** a species and region with no hand-made demo series gets one generated by Python. This covers
  the monthly first-sale price and volume, the season, storage profiles, market postings and quality records.
  - It is deterministic.
  - Its seasonal shape comes from a species-type pattern (`generation_patterns.csv`).
  - Its price level comes from real NOAA landings where they exist.
  - It is published as source `SRC-GEN-01` (tier C), so confidence is **low** and a caveat names where the price
    level came from.
  - The 64 hand-made series are unchanged, and all 53 golden cases still pass.
- **Unusual combinations** (a farm picking a wild species, a pot fisher picking salmon) are accepted with a caveat
  instead of an error.

## Your own question
- **Pick "Ask my own question..."** and type it. Python matches it to the nearest calculation
  (`question_cues.csv`), for example "when do prices peak" runs best months.
- **Answered separately:** the question itself is also answered under **Your question**, from real U.S. data, and
  from the web if ticked.
- **No match:** the operation's default calculation runs and the report says so.
- **Several species:** with more than one species, only sell-or-hold and buy-now-or-wait can run, because the
  rankings work on one species.

## Goals, constraints and the note
- **Your own goal and constraints:** you can type them, as well as picking from the lists. Instruction-like
  entries are dropped.
- **The agents now receive them.** Both the analyst and the summary writer get the goal, the constraints and the
  experience level. Before this change, they never reached the agents at all.
- **The analyst writes a "Your situation" lens note** that connects the result to your situation and to your
  question.
- **Python adds its own notes** for known cases, such as cash needed soon while the advice is to hold, or storage
  months shorter than the wait.
- **What Python reads from the note:** species, region (including states and ports), storage and months of
  storage, volume, customers, goal, experience, constraints, questions, and topics the scenario does not cover. The
  rules are in `note_cues.csv` and `place_regions.csv`.
  - The page shows "What we took from your note".
  - A **Fill the form from my note** button copies it into the form.
  - At run time the note adds constraints and fills an empty goal and experience. Where the note and the form
    disagree, the form wins and the report says so.
- **Topics outside the data** (loans, permits, insurance, hiring, gear) go to the web researcher when web research
  is ticked. When it isn't, the report points to the tick-box instead of just refusing.
- **What doesn't change:** the note and the question never change a number or the direction, and neither is
  written to the saved run.
